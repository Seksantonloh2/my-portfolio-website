FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Get in touch and a partner will reply within one business day.

What size of organisation do you work with?

We typically partner with mid-market and enterprise organisations, from fast-growing companies of 100 people to global groups of 50,000+. What matters most is a leadership team committed to change.

How long does a typical engagement last?

Diagnostic projects usually run 4–8 weeks. Full strategy and transformation programmes range from 3 to 12 months, and many clients retain us on an ongoing advisory basis.

How are your fees structured?

We offer fixed-fee projects, monthly retainers and outcome-linked models where part of our fee is tied to the results we deliver. We’ll recommend what fits best after our first conversation.

Who will actually work on our project?

Every engagement is led hands-on by a partner with 15+ years of experience, supported by a small senior team. No bait-and-switch with junior staff.
