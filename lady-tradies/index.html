Does it cost anything to find a tradie?

No. Searching the directory is free. You contact the tradeswoman yourself, and the price of the job is agreed between the two of you.

Are the tradies licensed?

Listings show the licence details each tradeswoman provides. For licensed work like electrical, plumbing and gas, always check her licence number with your state's licensing body before work starts. [Confirm verification policy with client]

How do I list my business?

Click "List my business", fill in your trade, service area and contact details, and add a few photos of your work. [Confirm listing fee / process with client]

I want to get into a trade. Where do I start?

Try the Getting Started quiz above, then read up on apprenticeships and pre-apprenticeship courses through TAFE in your state. Talking to a woman already in the trade helps too.

Can men use the directory to hire?

Of course. Anyone can search and hire. Every tradie listed in the directory is a woman.
