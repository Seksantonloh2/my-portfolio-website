Why we exist

Ask for her by name.

Plenty of people would rather have a woman in their home, or just want to back women in trades. Until we came along, there was nowhere to find her.

Lady Tradies started as a list of female tradespeople and has grown into a national directory. Every trade is covered, from carpentry and electrical to plumbing, tiling and landscaping. Tradeswomen also come to us for resources, networking and advice on starting out.
